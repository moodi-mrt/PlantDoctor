      What this means

      • Very accurate on clear, single-leaf photos similar to its training data.
      • Much less reliable on busy field scenes — retake close-up photos when the result is flagged.
      • Knows only the listed crops and diseases; anything else is mapped to the nearest match.
      • Confirm serious findings with an agronomist before treating.
